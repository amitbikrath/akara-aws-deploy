# Privacy Policy

AKARA (akara.studio and this store) respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect and why.

## What we collect
- Your name, shipping address, email, and phone number, so we can deliver your order and contact you about it.
- Payment details are collected by Razorpay, our payment processor. We never see or store your card or UPI details.
- Basic device and browsing data, such as pages viewed and IP address, through Shopify's standard analytics and cookies.
- Any message you send us by email or WhatsApp.

## How we use it
- To print, ship, and support your order.
- To send order and delivery updates.
- To send occasional news about new drops, only if you opt in. You can unsubscribe anytime.
- To improve the store and prevent fraud.

## Who we share it with
Only the partners needed to fulfil your order: Shopify (store platform), Razorpay (payments), our print and shipping partner (Qikink, India), and the courier. We do not sell your data to anyone.

## Cookies
We use cookies to keep your cart, remember your preferences, and understand how the store is used. You can block cookies in your browser, but checkout may not work without them.

## Your rights
You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email amit.rath@akara1.com. We reply within 7 business days.

## Data retention
We keep order records for as long as required by Indian tax and accounting law. Marketing data is deleted when you unsubscribe.
