# Refund and Replacement Policy

Every AKARA piece is printed to order, one at a time, just for you. Because of this, we do not offer cash refunds on printed goods. We do make things right when something goes wrong.

## Free replacement for defects
If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or with a print or fabric fault, we replace it free of charge.
- Tell us within 7 days of delivery.
- Send 2 to 3 clear photos of the issue and the order number to amit.rath@akara1.com or WhatsApp +91 99935 18528.
- We confirm within 2 business days and ship a replacement. You do not need to send the faulty piece back.

## Size exchange
Picked the wrong size? We exchange once, free, within 7 days of delivery.
- The tee must be unworn, unwashed, and with tags on.
- Message us with the order number and the size you need. We arrange the pickup.
- The new size ships once the original is picked up. Exchanges depend on the size being in stock with our print partner.

## What we cannot accept
- Returns for change of mind, or because the colour looks slightly different from your screen.
- Items that are worn, washed, altered, or without tags.
- Requests made more than 7 days after delivery.
- Cash refunds on any printed item.

## Cancellations
Orders go to print within 24 hours. Message us right away if you need to cancel. Once printing has started, the order cannot be cancelled.

## Refunds in rare cases
If we cannot replace a defective item, or an order is lost in transit and cannot be re-shipped, we refund the full amount to your original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.
