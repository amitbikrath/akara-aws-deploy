# Shipping Policy

## Where we ship
We currently ship only within India. International shipping is not available yet.

## Cost
Shipping is free on every order, to every pin code we serve.

## How long it takes
Each piece is printed to order. Printing takes 2 to 4 business days. Delivery takes 5 to 8 business days from the day you order. Remote pin codes may take a little longer.

## Tracking
You get an email with a tracking link when your order ships. You can also message us on WhatsApp at +91 99935 18528 with your order number.

## Payment
We accept prepaid payment only: UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, and wallets, through Razorpay. Cash on Delivery is not available right now.

## Failed deliveries
If the courier cannot reach you after repeated attempts, the parcel comes back to us. We will contact you to arrange a re-ship. A second re-ship may carry a courier charge.

## Wrong address
Check your address at checkout. If you spot an error, message us within 12 hours and we will correct it before the parcel ships.
