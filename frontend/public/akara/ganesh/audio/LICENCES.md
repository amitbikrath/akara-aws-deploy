## Notes
- Rejected: Wikimedia Commons "Category:Bells in Hindu worship" and "Category:Audio files of bells" — checked directly; no genuine Indian temple/ghanta audio recordings exist there (only church bells, school bells, Japanese rin, and photos of ghantas, no sound files).
- Rejected: Pixabay's "Temple Bells" (kalsstockmedia) and its "Church, Temple Bell, Gong, Dong" series — page marked **AI generated** and tagged Church/Cathedral rather than a real Hindu temple recording, so excluded per the "real temple bell" requirement.
- Not attempted: Freesound.org direct downloads — skipped per instructions (requires login); the two candidates sourced from Freesound were pulled via their Pixabay mirror instead, which needs no login and carries the Pixabay Content License.
- All files downloaded with curl using a browser User-Agent; direct CDN links were captured from the Pixabay page's actual playback network requests (Pixabay serves full, non-watermarked audio for sound effects without requiring login).
